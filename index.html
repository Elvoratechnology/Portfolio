Featured Creative Work • Story Writing

The One and Only

“Today, at the celebration, I feel confused about my feelings for His Majesty. I don’t know why he makes me feel this way, but I know that he resembles someone from my past.”

After that, he disappeared, and I did not see his face because it’s too dark.

Today, at the celebration, I feel confused about my feelings for His Majesty. I don’t know why he makes me feel this way, but I know that he resembles someone from my past.

Ohh my... what do I do now? I accidentally bumped into His Majesty, and I thought he would be angry with me, especially after all the bad things he heard about me from my stepmother and stepsister.

But instead of being angry, His Majesty tells me that it’s okay. His expression looks furios, but I don’t think it’s because of me. He isn’t looking at me, and I feel as if someone behind me is saying bad things about me.

I try to turn around, but His Majesty holds me and covers my eyes, saying that there’s no need to turn around.

My heart beats faster and faster. Something comes to my mind: I see a little boy with black hair and red eyes protecting my little self from Keah.

I have been standing still for about five minutes without noticing it. His Majesty calling my name brings me back to reality. When I look at him, I see the little boy in him. Then I realize that they’re the same person.

His Majesty is the little boy who was once my close childhood friend, the one who left me behind to take the throne at a young age after his parents died in a carriage accident.

If my father hadn’t told me the reason why he left, maybe I couldn’t have forgiven him.

Then I accidentally say his name, “Drick,” the nickname I gave him when we were both still kids. When he hears me say that name, he hugs me and says:

“I miss you. I really miss you. I’m glad that you remember me now. I’m sorry for leaving you behind without any trace...”

It’s been weeks since the celebration party, but I still can’t forget His Majesty’s face. During dinner with the empress and my stepsister, they continue to make fun of me and humiliate me in front of His Majesty.

I wonder why His Majesty doesn’t pay attention to their words. He doesn’t seem interested in their insults, but instead, he seems focused on something else.

I feel confused about my real feelings for him. The only thing I know is that he reminds me of someone I was once close to.

While practicing fire magic, I accidentally bump into His Majesty. I expect him to be angry because of the bad things my stepmother and stepsister have said about me, but he tells me that it’s okay.

Then he covers my eyes when I feel someone behind me talking badly about me. My heart begins beating faster, and memories of a little boy with black hair and red eyes come back to me.

I realize that His Majesty is the same little boy who once protected me, my childhood friend who left to take the throne after his parents died.

When I accidentally call him “Drick,” the nickname I gave him as a child, he hugs me and tells me that he misses me. He is glad that I remember him and apologizes for leaving me behind without a trace.

Now, I understand that the person I have been remembering is the same person standing before me.

He is my childhood friend, my one and only hope, and the person who gives me the strength to continue living.

In the end, both Ferilyn and Hedrick rule their two powerful empires peacefully.
Blog // Sharing What I Learn

MY BLOG.

Thoughts on reading, continuous learning, and finding confidence as a student.

Welcome Post • Student Reflections 3 min read

Welcome to my blog

Hi everyone welcome to my blog. This is simply my way of sharing what I’ve learned over the years. I found that I learn better by communicating my knowledge to others. Reading books can often be very daunting and I find that I can get a little line stuck in my head which then gets me to think all day. By communicating I can share this feeling.

I have found that getting into random topics helps me. It helps reduce stress around learning. As you are doing it out of genuine interest you feel less pressure. You have more to say in conversations, feel a bit more confident in speaking in class and meet other people who are interested in the same thing.

If you’re a student like me (still finding your way) this is for you! Don’t worry about having a perfect plan just keep turning the page and asking questions. Your ideas are important and you should be heard. Let’s learn together!

#ContinuousLearning #Communication #StudentPerspectives
